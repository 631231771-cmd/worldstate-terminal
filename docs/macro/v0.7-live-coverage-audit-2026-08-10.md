## Interpretation

The current runtime is operational for a narrow public-data slice: ECB/BoE
macro context, deterministic state snapshots, one observed daily gold series,
and manual pre-T0 consensus/market CSV imports. It is not yet a US macro event
terminal or a multi-asset live market terminal. The next evidence-bearing work
is successful BLS/FRED US capture, official BOJ/China input, and additional
observed market instruments; none should be represented by fixtures or proxy
labels as if they were original data.
