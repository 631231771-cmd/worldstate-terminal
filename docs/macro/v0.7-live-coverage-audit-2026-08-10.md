## Interpretation

The current runtime is operational for a narrow public-data slice: BLS
current-state growth/inflation, ECB/BoE macro context, deterministic state
snapshots, one observed daily gold series, and manual pre-T0 consensus/market
CSV imports. It is not yet a US macro event terminal or a multi-asset live
market terminal: BLS rows cannot reconstruct historical first prints, and the
policy/rates/liquidity dimensions still have no observed US source. The next
evidence-bearing work is FRED/Fed time series, official BOJ/China input, and
additional observed market instruments; none should be represented by
fixtures or proxy labels as if they were original data.
